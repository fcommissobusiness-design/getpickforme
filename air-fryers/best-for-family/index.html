Best air fryer for a family of 4 or 5

Cooking for a family changes the answer. You need enough capacity for a main and a side at once, which for most families means a dual basket.

What a family actually needs

For four or five people, the bottleneck isn't total volume, it's getting everything ready together. A single basket makes you cook in sequence: chips, then chicken, by which time the chips are cold. That's why families gravitate to dual baskets.

The family picks

  • The default: Ninja Foodi Dual Zone AF400UK. 9.5L across two drawers, and the Sync function lands both foods at the same time. It's the one most families settle on.
  • Lower budget: Tefal Easy Fry Dual. Two drawers for less, if the Ninja is a stretch.
  • Prefer one big basket: Instant Vortex Plus 6L. Simpler, roomy, if you don't need two temperatures at once.

What to skip for a family

Anything under 5L. A 3.8L single basket is a fine machine, but for four people you'll be cooking three rounds and resenting it. Buy for the household you have.

Not sure on capacity? Start with the size guide.

Common questions

What is the best air fryer for a family of 4?

A dual-basket around 7 to 9L. The Ninja Foodi Dual Zone AF400UK (9.5L, two drawers) is the common family default because it cooks a main and a side to finish together. The Tefal Easy Fry Dual is the lower-budget alternative.

Can one air fryer cook a full family meal?

A dual-basket can: one drawer for your main, one for the side, finishing at the same time. A single basket can too, but you'll cook in sequence, which is slower for a family.
