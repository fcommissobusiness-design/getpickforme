Dehumidifiers guide

Best dehumidifier for drying clothes indoors

Drying washing indoors in winter is one of the biggest causes of damp and mould. A dehumidifier fixes it, dries the load faster, and costs pennies to run.

Why a dehumidifier beats a radiator

Drying clothes on radiators pumps litres of water into the air, which lands on cold walls and windows as condensation and, eventually, mould. A dehumidifier pulls that moisture straight back out, so the washing dries faster and the damp never settles. Run it in a closed room with the airer and it can cut drying time dramatically for a few pennies of electricity.

  • Best in a cold room: Meaco DD8L. A desiccant that dries washing fast even in an unheated room, where a compressor would stall.

Heated room or cold room decides it

If you dry clothes in a normal heated room, a compressor unit like the Pro Breeze is the efficient choice. If the room is cold (a utility room, garage or unheated spare room), a desiccant like the Meaco keeps working and gently warms the air, so it actually dries faster there.

Does a dehumidifier dry clothes faster?

Yes. In a closed room with an airer, a dehumidifier pulls the moisture straight out of the air, so clothes dry noticeably faster than on an airer alone, and far more cheaply than tumble drying. It also stops that moisture settling on cold walls and windows as condensation and mould.
