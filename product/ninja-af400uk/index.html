Common questions

How does the Ninja AF400UK Sync function actually work?

You set two different cook programmes (for example chicken thighs in one drawer, chips in the other, with different times and temperatures) and hit Sync. The air fryer automatically delays the start of the shorter programme so both drawers finish at the same moment. It's the feature owners actually keep coming back to: weekday meals land together without juggling timers.

What are the real dimensions of the Ninja AF400UK, and will it fit under kitchen cabinets?

The AF400UK measures roughly 41 cm wide × 38 cm deep × 32 cm tall. It's large: owners consistently warn to measure your worktop first. It fits under most standard-height UK wall cabinets (around 45 cm clearance) but leaves little room for the lid to open fully. A smaller single-basket like the Cosori Lite 38 is a better fit for tight worktops.

Is the Ninja AF400UK dishwasher-safe, and what's the easiest way to clean it?

Yes, both non-stick baskets and the crisper plates are dishwasher-safe, and this is one of the features owners praise most. For daily use most people wipe them down with hot soapy water instead (the coating lasts longer that way). The outer housing just needs a damp cloth; no removable heating element to worry about.

Does the Ninja AF400UK actually save energy compared to an oven?

For small-to-medium portions, yes. Ninja claim up to 75% less energy than a full oven, and the practical reason is simple: no preheat (meals start cooking instantly) and a far smaller cavity to heat. Real-world test figures from UK consumer publications land at 40-60% energy savings versus an electric oven for the same meal. For big roasts or a full tray of veg it's still cheaper to use the oven once.

What's the warranty on the Ninja AF400UK, and is the extended warranty worth it?

Standard Ninja warranty is one year. By registering the product on ninjakitchen.co.uk within 28 days of purchase you extend it to five years (free). Do it: a thread of 12+ month failure reports exists in reviews, and the extended registration is the single most important step to protect your investment. Keep the Amazon receipt too.
