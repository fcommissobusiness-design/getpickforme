Cordless vacuums guide

Best cordless vacuum for pet hair

With pets, the brush roll is everything. The right cordless stops hair wrapping and tangling; the wrong one turns every clean into a scissors job.

Why pet homes need a different answer

Pet hair does two annoying things: it wraps tightly around the spinning brush, and it fills the bin fast. The fix isn't more suction, it's an anti-hair-wrap brush design and an easy-empty bin. Get those right and a cordless is a joy with pets; get them wrong and you'll dread it.

The picks for pet hair

  • Best for hair, best value: Shark Stratos Anti Hair Wrap. The anti-hair-wrap brush genuinely stops the tangle, and two batteries get a whole house done in one pass.
  • Best overall if budget allows: Dyson V15 Detect. The strongest suction here plus a hair-handling brush, ideal if you also have allergies.
  • Lighter option: Dyson V8. Lighter to carry around after a shedding dog, still copes well with hair on hard floors and low carpet.

The feature that matters most

If you remember one thing: prioritise the anti-hair-wrap brush over raw suction. It's the difference between emptying the bin and spending ten minutes with scissors on the brush roll.

Common questions

What is the best cordless vacuum for pet hair in the UK?

For most pet owners the Shark Stratos Anti Hair Wrap is the best value: its anti-hair-wrap brush stops hair tangling, and two batteries do a whole house. If budget allows, the Dyson V15 Detect adds the strongest suction and is better for allergy sufferers.

Do I need special features for pet hair?

Yes, one in particular: an anti-hair-wrap brush roll. It's more important than suction, because it stops pet hair wrapping around the brush and clogging it. An easy-empty bin and good filtration help too, especially for allergies.
