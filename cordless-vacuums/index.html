Which cordless vacuum is right for you?

Best cordless vacuum UK: how to choose one (2026)

Cordless vacuums all claim huge suction and endless runtime. Neither number tells you which one suits your home. Three real questions do.

Three questions settle it

  1. How big is your home? This sets the runtime you need. A flat is fine on 30 to 40 minutes; a three-bed house with carpet wants 50+ or a second battery.
  2. How much does it weigh in your hand? You hold a cordless up, not push it along. A heavy one is tiring on stairs and when you reach up for cobwebs. Weight matters more than people expect.
  3. Pets or carpet? Pet hair and carpet are what separate a good cordless from a frustrating one. If you have either, the brush head and anti-tangle design matter more than headline suction.

Runtime: read it honestly

The big runtime numbers are almost always in the lowest "eco" mode. On a powered carpet setting you'll get a fraction of that. Rule of thumb: take the headline figure, halve it for real carpet cleaning, and check that still covers your floor space. If it's close, get a model with a removable or second battery.

Weight: the number nobody prints big

Anything around 2.5kg feels light; past 3kg you notice it on stairs and overhead. If you've a lot of stairs or limited grip, prioritise weight over everything, even if it costs you some runtime.

Pets and long hair: the brush is everything

If hair wraps around the brush roll, you'll spend as long cutting it free as vacuuming. Anti-hair-wrap brush designs (and Dyson's and Shark's heads in particular) are the real fix. This is the one area where spending more genuinely pays off for pet owners.

What matters less than the box suggests

  • Headline suction (AW/Pa). Above a sensible threshold, more suction mostly drains the battery faster. Brush design moves more dirt than raw numbers.
  • Accessory count. You'll use the crevice tool and the motorised head. The rest stays in a drawer.
  • App connectivity. A vacuum does not need an app.

Head-to-head: Dyson V8 vs Dyson V15 Detect

Common questions

What is the best cordless vacuum in the UK?

There's no single best; it depends on your home. For most people the Dyson V8 is the sensible default: light, strong and far cheaper than the flagship. Pet owners should look at the Shark Stratos for its anti-hair-wrap brush, and anyone who wants the deepest clean and has the budget should consider the Dyson V15 Detect.

How much runtime do I really need in a cordless vacuum?

For a flat, 30 to 40 minutes is plenty. For a three-bed house with carpet, aim for 50+ minutes or a model with a removable or second battery, because powered carpet cleaning drains the battery far faster than the headline 'eco' figure.

Does suction (air watts) matter most when buying a cordless vacuum?

No. Above a sensible level, more suction mainly shortens runtime. Brush-head design, weight and anti-hair-wrap features make a bigger difference to how clean your floors actually get.
